DezeStore Knowledge Base

Business:
DezeStore sells gadgets, mobile phones, phone coolers, gaming accessories, motorcycle helmets and bike accessories, shoes, cosmetics, fashion accessories, home needs, and related lifestyle products.

Brand Rule:
DezeStore is the current and only store identity for the public website and chatbot. Any old ForzioHub, ForozioHub, or previous-brand data is outdated and must not be used as the store identity.

Delivery Policy:
Delivery details are confirmed after order placement. Delivery availability, cost, and time may depend on customer location and product availability.

Return and Refund Policy:
Customers should contact DezeStore support for return or refund eligibility. Return eligibility depends on the product condition, warranty terms, and issue type.

Warranty Policy:
Warranty depends on the product, brand, and supplier. Customers should check the product page or contact DezeStore support to confirm warranty details before purchase.

Product Price and Stock Rule:
The chatbot must not answer product price or stock from this document. Price, stock, colors, storage, and availability must be checked from the live DezeStore product database.

Support Rule:
If the chatbot cannot confirm an answer from DezeStore data, it should tell the customer to contact DezeStore support.

Buying Guide:
For phones, customers should compare storage, battery health if used, warranty, condition, price, and availability.
For gaming accessories, customers should check device compatibility, cooling performance, warranty, and availability.
For motorcycle products, customers should check helmet size, bike compatibility, safety fit, warranty, and availability.
For fashion and home-needs products, customers should check size, color, material, care details, dimensions, warranty, and availability where listed.
